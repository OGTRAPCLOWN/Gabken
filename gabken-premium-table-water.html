About Gabken

Pure hydration, exceptional quality — since day one

Gabken Premium Table Water is a trusted provider of premium drinking water and beverages, committed to purity, health, quality, and reliable delivery.

Our story

From a single purification plant to a nationwide brand

Gabken began with a simple belief: everyone deserves access to genuinely pure, safe drinking water. What started as a single processing facility has grown into a trusted supplier for homes, hotels, hospitals and offices across the country — without ever compromising on our purification standards.

2008

Gabken founded with our first table water processing line.

2013

NAFDAC & SON certification achieved; sachet water line launched.

2017

Expanded into soft drinks, juices and corporate bulk supply.

2021

Nationwide distribution network reaches 12 states.

2026

Launched online store, distributor program and custom event water.
